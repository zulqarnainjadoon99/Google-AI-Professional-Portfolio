# Course 03 — AI for Research and Insights
## AI-Assisted Research, Source Verification & Business Insights

### Course Overview
This course focused on using AI to support research, compare information, evaluate sources, identify business insights, and prepare evidence-aware professional reports.

## Task 05: AI-Assisted Research & Source Verification

**Objective:** Use AI to research business software options, compare their features, and assess the reliability of information before making recommendations.

**Practical Scenario:** Comparing Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace for a 15-person IT services company.

**Work Completed:**
- Defined the organization's collaboration and productivity requirements.
- Researched Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.
- Compared key areas, including:
  - Email and business communication.
  - Document creation and collaboration.
  - Cloud storage and file sharing.
  - Team meetings and communication.
  - Administration and access management.
  - Security and productivity features.
- Reviewed source information and identified claims requiring additional verification.
- Prepared a corrected research report with a source-verification audit.
- Separated verified information from estimates, assumptions, and details that may change.

**Quality and Research Review:**
Software prices, storage allowances, licensing terms, and included features depend on subscription plans, region, and current vendor policies. These details should be checked against official vendor documentation before purchasing.

**Skills Demonstrated:** AI-assisted research, source verification, comparative analysis, information evaluation, and professional report writing.

## Task 06: AI-Powered Market Research & Business Insights

**Objective:** Explore a business market using AI-assisted research and develop insights that can support informed business planning.

**Practical Scenario:** Researching the IT support services market for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) in Pakistan.

**Work Completed:**
- Explored the digitalization of small and medium-sized businesses in Pakistan.
- Examined potential demand for IT support services.
- Identified relevant service categories, including:
  - Helpdesk and technical support.
  - Network maintenance and troubleshooting.
  - Computer and workstation support.
  - Software and cloud productivity assistance.
  - Basic cybersecurity support.
- Reviewed competitors and service providers, including Trubyte, OSITS, ZealsTECH, RSM Pakistan, and Gentec.
- Identified possible market opportunities and business considerations.
- Prepared a market research report with clear separation of verified facts, external estimates, and assumptions.

**Quality and Market Review:**
Competitor names and market observations are research starting points, not proof of current market share or service quality. Pricing and demand estimates were not treated as verified facts without supporting evidence. Further validation through official company information, customer research, and direct quotations is necessary.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Market research, competitor analysis, business insights, evidence evaluation, strategic thinking, and AI-assisted reporting.

## Tools and Methodology

**AI Tool:** Google Gemini

**Research Workflow:**
1. Define the research question.
2. Identify relevant topics and information sources.
3. Generate an initial research outline.
4. Compare information and evaluate source reliability.
5. Separate verified facts from estimates and assumptions.
6. Identify opportunities, limitations, and business insights.
7. Prepare a corrected professional report.
