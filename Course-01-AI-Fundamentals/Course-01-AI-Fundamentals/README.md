# Course 01 — AI Fundamentals
## AI Prompt Engineering, Output Evaluation & Responsible AI Use

### Course Overview
This course focused on understanding AI fundamentals, improving prompt quality, evaluating AI-generated responses, and applying responsible AI practices in professional IT environments.

## Task 01: Prompt Intelligence Lab — AI Prompt Engineering & Output Evaluation

**Objective:** Explore how different prompt structures influence AI-generated results and evaluate the quality of responses.

**Practical Scenario:** Creating a seven-day IT Support Training Plan.

**Work Completed:**
- Created and compared basic, structured, and expert-role prompts.
- Evaluated how context, role instructions, and clear requirements improve AI responses.
- Developed a structured seven-day training plan covering:
  - IT support ticket workflows and SLAs.
  - Multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  - Workstation deployment and operating system administration.
  - Network troubleshooting fundamentals.
  - Cloud productivity tools.
  - Professional communication and practical assessment.

**Quality and Safety Review:**
Technical instructions were reviewed critically. Network commands and troubleshooting actions should only be performed after proper diagnosis, authorization, and consideration of potential risks.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Prompt engineering, response comparison, AI output evaluation, technical reasoning, and instruction optimization.

## Task 02: AI Output Evaluation & Responsible AI Security Procedures

**Objective:** Identify risks in AI-generated security advice and develop responsible procedures for handling confidential information.

**Practical Scenario:** Reviewing unsafe AI recommendations about uploading confidential company documents to public AI tools and assuming that deleting a chat completely removes the associated risk.

**Work Completed:**

Two professional documentation deliverables were developed:

1. **AI Acceptable Use Policy (AUP):**
   - Approved AI tools and permitted use.
   - Restrictions on uploading confidential information.
   - Data classification and privacy considerations.
   - Human verification of AI-generated information.
   - Responsible and secure AI usage.

2. **AI Security Incident Reporting Procedure:**
   - Identify and report accidental data exposure.
   - Notify the appropriate security team.
   - Assess information sensitivity and potential impact.
   - Contain the incident and take corrective action.
   - Rotate exposed credentials or API keys where necessary.
   - Document the incident and provide awareness training.

**Quality and Safety Review:**
Closing a browser tab or deleting a conversation does not guarantee complete removal of exposed information. Organizations must verify the relevant AI provider's data retention policies and follow their incident response requirements.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Responsible AI usage, security awareness, risk assessment, policy documentation, incident reporting, and professional technical writing.
