# Course 04 — AI for Writing and Communicating
## AI-Assisted Professional IT Communication & Incident Reporting

### Course Overview
This course focused on using AI to improve professional communication, prepare clear workplace messages, document technical incidents, and communicate IT issues effectively.

## Task 07: AI-Assisted Professional IT Support Communication

**Objective:** Use AI to prepare professional, clear, and customer-focused communication for IT support situations.

**Practical Scenario:** Responding to a client complaint about an office network and Wi-Fi connection remaining unstable for two days.

**Work Completed:**
- Reviewed the client's complaint and identified the main communication requirements.
- Prepared a professional response acknowledging the inconvenience.
- Included an appropriate apology and reassurance.
- Organized a preliminary troubleshooting approach.
- Drafted a proposed service update and expected communication timeline.
- Prepared a WhatsApp-friendly message for quick client communication.
- Included escalation and follow-up communication.
- Developed a resolution communication template for use after the issue is actually resolved.

**Quality and Communication Review:**
The communication was reviewed to avoid promising a confirmed resolution time without technical assessment. Support staff should not claim that checks have been completed or an issue has been resolved unless those actions have actually taken place.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Professional writing, customer communication, complaint handling, service updates, escalation communication, and AI-assisted message drafting.

## Task 08: AI-Assisted IT Incident Reporting & Documentation

**Objective:** Use AI to prepare structured technical incident reports that clearly document service disruptions, impact, investigation, and follow-up actions.

**Practical Scenario:** Documenting an office network outage lasting approximately two hours, with a switch configuration issue considered a possible cause.

**Work Completed:**
- Prepared a structured IT incident report.
- Documented the incident overview and approximate duration.
- Described the affected network service and potential business impact.
- Recorded the suspected technical cause.
- Organized investigation and troubleshooting details.
- Included proposed corrective and preventive actions.
- Structured the report for professional review and future reference.

**Quality and Incident Review:**
The switch configuration issue remains a suspected cause unless supported by configuration records, system logs, or technical investigation. A professional incident report must distinguish confirmed observations from assumptions and unresolved findings.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Incident documentation, technical reporting, root-cause analysis concepts, problem communication, corrective action planning, and professional IT documentation.

## Tools and Methodology

**AI Tool:** Google Gemini

**Workflow:**
1. Understand the communication or incident scenario.
2. Identify the intended audience and purpose.
3. Generate an initial draft or report structure.
4. Improve clarity, tone, and organization.
5. Review technical accuracy and unsupported claims.
6. Correct assumptions and distinguish confirmed facts.
7. Prepare the final professional document.

## Learning Outcomes

- Write professional IT support messages.
- Communicate clearly with customers and colleagues.
- Handle complaints with appropriate language and empathy.
- Prepare structured incident reports.
- Document technical issues and their potential impact.
- Distinguish suspected causes from confirmed root causes.
- Use AI to improve workplace communication and documentation.
