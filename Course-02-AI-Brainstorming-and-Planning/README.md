# Course 02 — AI for Brainstorming and Planning
## AI-Powered Business Brainstorming & Strategic Project Planning

### Course Overview
This course focused on using AI to generate business ideas, explore opportunities, organize plans, and support practical project coordination in an IT services environment.

## Task 03: AI-Powered Business Brainstorming & Strategic Planning

**Objective:** Use AI to explore IT service business opportunities and develop a practical strategy for launching and organizing an IT support service.

**Practical Scenario:** Planning an IT support service business for small and medium-sized organizations.

**Work Completed:**
- Generated and reviewed ten potential IT service business ideas.
- Compared ideas based on service demand, feasibility, and business potential.
- Explored service offerings such as:
  - IT helpdesk and technical support.
  - Network and connectivity support.
  - Workstation setup and maintenance.
  - Software installation and user assistance.
  - Basic cybersecurity awareness.
- Developed an initial operational approach using ticket management, Kanban task tracking, and end-of-day team check-ins.
- Prepared a proposed 30-day rollout plan.

**Quality and Business Review:**
The business plan was reviewed for practical feasibility. Customer information must be protected, access to internal documentation must be controlled, and service pricing and business targets should be validated before real-world implementation.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Business brainstorming, strategic planning, idea evaluation, service design, and AI-assisted decision-making.

## Task 04: AI-Assisted IT Project Planning & Coordination

**Objective:** Develop a structured IT project plan using AI assistance, including tasks, responsibilities, resources, timelines, and risk management.

**Practical Scenario:** Planning the IT setup for a 15-employee office within a proposed 30-day project timeline.

**Work Completed:**
- Defined project scope and objectives.
- Divided the project into manageable implementation phases.
- Identified team responsibilities and coordination requirements.
- Prepared an equipment and resource planning outline.
- Developed a preliminary budget structure.
- Identified potential project risks and mitigation measures.
- Established milestones and a proposed 30-day implementation schedule.
- Reviewed contingency planning, cloud-first identity management, least-privilege access, backups, and hardware sizing.

**Quality and Project Review:**
The proposed budget and equipment requirements require supplier quotations and technical assessment. A contingency reserve should be included, and access permissions should follow the principle of least privilege. Backup and recovery arrangements should be tested.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Project coordination, task planning, resource management, risk assessment, budgeting, scheduling, and AI-assisted documentation.

## Tools and Methodology

**AI Tool:** Google Gemini

**Workflow:**
1. Define the business or project challenge.
2. Generate ideas and possible approaches.
3. Compare options and evaluate feasibility.
4. Organize tasks, resources, and responsibilities.
5. Develop timelines and milestones.
6. Review risks, assumptions, and recommendations.
7. Refine the final professional plan.

## Learning Outcomes

- Use AI for structured brainstorming and idea generation.
- Evaluate business opportunities and service concepts.
- Develop practical strategic plans.
- Organize IT projects into manageable phases.
- Identify project resources, responsibilities, and risks.
- Create realistic timelines and milestones.
- Apply human judgment when reviewing AI-generated plans.
