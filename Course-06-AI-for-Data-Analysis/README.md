# Course 06 — AI for Data Analysis
## AI-Assisted IT Support Data Analysis & Business Intelligence

### Course Overview
This course focused on using AI to analyze operational data, interpret performance indicators, identify trends, and develop business intelligence reports and dashboard plans for IT support operations.

## Task 11: AI-Assisted IT Support Data Analysis & Business Performance Reporting

**Objective:** Analyze IT support performance data and prepare a structured report highlighting operational results, trends, and improvement opportunities.

**Practical Scenario:** Reviewing a simulated four-week IT support dataset for a technology support department.

**Work Completed:**
- Analyzed a simulated dataset containing 205 support tickets.
- Reviewed ticket resolution performance and operational indicators.
- Calculated and interpreted key metrics:
  - Total tickets: 205.
  - Resolved tickets: 177.
  - Overall resolution rate: 86.34%.
  - Average of weekly reported resolution times: 7.85 hours.
  - Average of weekly reported customer satisfaction scores: 86.25%.
  - Critical-priority tickets: 18.
- Reviewed weekly performance patterns.
- Identified possible operational pressure points and areas for improvement.
- Prepared a professional IT Support Department Performance Report.

**Quality and Data Review:**
The dataset is simulated and does not represent verified company operations. The 7.85-hour resolution time and 86.25% customer satisfaction figures are averages of weekly reported values, not necessarily weighted averages across individual tickets. The sum of weekly pending counts is not the same as the final outstanding backlog. Correlation between operational indicators does not establish causation. A 90% customer satisfaction target was treated as a proposed benchmark.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Data analysis, KPI interpretation, performance reporting, trend analysis, numerical review, and AI-assisted business insights.

## Task 12: AI-Assisted IT Performance Dashboard & Business Intelligence Blueprint

**Objective:** Design a practical dashboard blueprint to help IT managers monitor support performance and make data-informed operational decisions.

**Practical Scenario:** Planning an IT support performance dashboard based on the simulated support department dataset.

**Work Completed:**
- Identified key operational KPIs for management.
- Prepared a dashboard layout and reporting structure.
- Proposed visualizations for:
  - Total and resolved tickets.
  - Open and pending tickets.
  - Ticket priority distribution.
  - Resolution performance.
  - Customer satisfaction.
  - Weekly operational trends.
- Suggested filters for date ranges, ticket categories, priorities, and status.
- Prepared a proposed Looker Studio implementation guide.
- Explained how dashboard users could review performance and identify areas requiring attention.

**Quality and Dashboard Review:**
This deliverable is a dashboard blueprint, not a live implemented dashboard. KPI definitions, data sources, refresh frequency, and calculation rules should be finalized before implementation. Simulated figures must be clearly labelled in any demonstration.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Business intelligence planning, dashboard design, KPI selection, data visualization planning, reporting, and management-oriented analysis.
